Colour options for Deseret SACCO

Based on measuring the colours on 41 Kenyan SACCO websites and 38 banks, microfinance lenders, mobile-money apps and insurers. Each option below shows your Growing comb logo, the website and the member portal in that palette.

46%of SACCOs use green as their main colour (19 of 41), as do Co-op Bank, KCB and M-Pesa.
21brands use blue or navy as their main colour, usually with an orange or red accent.
0 of 79brands use plum, purple or aubergine as their main colour. This is open territory.
3 of 41SACCOs use a teal (Kencream, Elimu, Smart Life). No bank does.

My recommendation

Plum & honey if you want to be the only one: nobody in Kenyan finance uses plum, and honey on plum looks warm and premium. Teal & honey if you want calm and safe while still standing apart from the green crowd. Whichever you choose, every logo file and the whole website switch over together.
